WiniOS Studio

Build, sign and run your SwiftUI apps on your iPhone — from Windows, no Mac needed.

A one-click assistant prepares the Swift toolchain, signing and your device. An integrated pipeline installs and verifies your app on your iPhone with evidence at every phase. Powered by xtool (MIT).

What it does

🧙 One-click setupWSL → Swift 6.4 → xtool → SDK (your Xcode.xip) → Apple ID → iPhone bridge. Idempotent, self-repairing, isolated distro.
🚀 Run on my iPhonebuild → sign → package → connect → install → verify, with real phase logs.
🔎 Honest statesInstalled is not opened. Errors come with the next action, never invented progress.
🖥️ VS Code readyOne click opens the project in VS Code — remote server + Swift extension + build server.

Legal

Apple's Xcode & Apple SDKs agreement forbids installing Apple SDKs on non-Apple-branded hardware. WiniOS Studio redistributes nothing from Apple: you download your own Xcode.xip with your own Apple ID and sign with your own credentials — the same approach as xtool. Use at your own risk with free/personal accounts; extra caution with corporate Apple IDs. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple Inc.
